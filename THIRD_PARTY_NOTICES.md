# Third-party dependencies

This repository does not vendor third-party source code. Rust dependencies and
their exact resolved versions are recorded in `Cargo.lock` and are downloaded
by Cargo from their declared registries. Each dependency remains subject to
its own upstream license and notices.

Redistributors of compiled artifacts are responsible for preserving the
licenses and notices required by the exact dependency set they distribute.
The Apache-2.0 license in this repository applies to user-authored Monitor and
Pulse material; it does not replace third-party terms.
