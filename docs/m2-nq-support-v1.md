# Local M2 NQ support v1

Pulse owns qualified support for exactly `nq.systemd_unit.postcondition/v1`
and `nq.http_endpoint.postcondition/v1` under their existing operator-beta
profiles. NQ owns acquisition, retained admission and diagnostic meaning;
Nightshift consumes the existing `nightshift.qualified_support.v1` answer.
Support does not authorize an effect or convert unqualified UTC into a bound.

Only the Ubuntu22.04 local two-VM, non-production M2 fixture is admitted:
`constellation-beta-http-fixture.service`, loaded/active/running/disabled, and
numeric private/loopback IPv4 GET port18080 `/healthz`, status200 and exact
shipped body hash, with redirects refused. Subject, target VM/unit bytes,
controller vantage, profile/question/version, semantic identity and threshold
policy are bound exactly. Different propositions and later generations need
reassessment; this is not a generic evaluator or production service.

A root-controlled `pulse.m2_support_config.v1` pins the NQ executable/config
bytes, watcher, VM machine/boot identity, M2 generation, immutable baseline
artifact bytes and Nightshift observation/input basis. All custody paths and
ancestors are root-owned, without group/other writes or symlinks. Same-root,
NQ/OS correctness and exclusive deployment ownership are explicit premises;
this local contract provides neither independent failure domains nor remote
attestation. No new signing credentials are needed.

`pulse-m2-support acquire --config ABSOLUTE_PATH` creates an exclusive permanent
claim, generates an internal random acquisition identity, and invokes NQ's
current `diagnostics acquire-next-local` operation. It must produce a different
artifact/run from the baseline. NQ export/qualify reopen the exact live bytes and
retained admission. The receipt stores exact acquisition/artifact/admission,
proposition/VM/config identity, NQ observed-at testimony and Linux boot-clock
start/end. The caller cannot supply an authored observation as acquisition.
An interruption preserves the claim: do not replay that enrollment to mint
new freshness. Retain it and explicitly enroll a new acquisition.

Acquisition including verification must finish within30 seconds. Expiry is
exclusive at60,000 milliseconds from acquisition **start**, on the same
boot-bound `/proc/uptime` clock. NQ wall-clock timestamps remain testimony and
are not used for freshness. A reboot, receipt/config change or clock reversal
refuses. This does not establish UTC accuracy or cross-VM clock comparison.

`pulse-m2-support resolve --config ABSOLUTE_PATH` reads one bounded Nightshift
query on stdin. It never acquires or refreshes evidence. It reopens the baseline
and support artifact through the pinned NQ verifier and exactly binds the live
query/nonce/cycle/proposition. Equal complete NQ conditions earn Current while
the receipt is current. Disagreement is Contradictory with exact references;
missing/incomplete/refused evidence is Unknown; equality at expiry is Expired.
Malformed, substituted or mismatched evidence refuses without positive support.
Historical artifact qualification alone never earns Current.

The receipt/answer are ordinary product records, not a campaign certificate.
A source-free installation needs the inert Debian package, exact NQ package,
operator-owned enrollment and fresh acquisition. The deployment runbook owns
fixture paths/setup; no target-specific application facts belong here.
